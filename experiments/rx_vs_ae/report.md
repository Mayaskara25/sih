## What these numbers say

**`kernel_rx` leads HAD100 (0.9713) and `crd` leads ABU (0.9674).** No single
detector wins both, which is itself worth reporting: the ranking is
dataset-dependent, and a paper claiming one universal winner from either set
alone would be overfitting to it.

**`fused` ranks 4th of 5 on HAD100 with the FULL 4-component set**
(`ace+index+rx+spatial`, verified per row, not assumed). HAD100 is the only
benchmark shipping real wavelengths (D13.4), so it is the only place the
`index` component can run at all — this was fusion's best chance and it did
not take it. Combined with D25's held-out weight search on ABU (+0.0018 macro
AUC over the best fixed component, with the optimizer assigning ACE exactly
0.0), **the report must not claim fusion beats its components.** What fusion
legitimately offers is comparable performance without a per-scene detector
choice, since the per-scene oracle is unavailable at inference.

**`local_rx` is last on HAD100 (0.9004)** despite using the window §3A.2
prescribes for 64x64 patches. It beats `global_rx` decisively on individual
scenes (0.9647 vs 0.8202 on `abu-airport-1`), so §3A.2's accept criterion
holds per scene and fails on the macro average. Both facts belong in any table
citing it.

**Macro exceeds micro for every detector on every dataset.** That is the
density effect made visible: performance is systematically worse on the
anomaly-dense scenes that dominate a pixel-weighted pool. A single "pooled
AUC" would quote the lower number without saying so.

**PR-AUC is far below ROC-AUC throughout** (0.41-0.65 vs 0.90-0.97). At
anomaly fractions under 5 %, ROC-AUC is the flattering metric and PR-AUC the
informative one. Both are reported for that reason, and the gap is wider on
ABU (0.32-0.43) than HAD100 (0.41-0.65).

**`crd` costs ~50 s and ~1 GB per scene** against `global_rx`'s 0.14 s and
71 MB — a ~360x runtime difference for +0.02 AUC on ABU and *worse* AUC on
HAD100. For the edge arm (§0.3, no CUDA at inference) that trade is decisive.

## Not covered here

- **`streaming_rx`** — takes a scene path, not a cube (`PATH_DETECTORS`), and
  its value is peak RSS rather than AUC. Its module documents that `.mat`
  cannot stream, which is most of this set.
- **Stage-1 recall and the false-negative audit** live in
  `experiments/cascade_recall_audit/` per §8 Level 1 — every missed labelled
  component with its extent, mean and max score, and the margin by which it
  missed the threshold. §13 rule 9 requires stage-1 recall separately from
  end-to-end accuracy.
- **Learned models** are scored on HAD100 only (O8/D13.4) and are not in this
  table, which is classical detectors. See `experiments/seg_arch/`.
